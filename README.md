**FocusHub** is a productivity and wellness app designed to help users manage their tasks, time, and emotions in one place. With draggable widgets, a Pomodoro timer, sticky notes, a mood tracker, breathing exercises, and challenges.

**Features**
- **Draggable Widgets:** Customize your workspace by dragging widgets like tasks, timers, and sticky notes.

- **Pomodoro Timer:** Boost productivity with focused work sessions and timed breaks.

- **Mood Tracker:** Log your mood daily and view your emotional well-being over time with a color-coded calendar.

- **Sticky Notes:** Jot down quick ideas or reminders with digital sticky notes.

- **Responsive Design:** Seamlessly switch between desktop and mobile views. Widgets stack vertically on mobile for easy access.

- **Customizable Settings:** Toggle light/dark mode, adjust timer settings, and reset widget positions with ease.

- **Breathing Exercise:** Practice mindfulness with a guided breathing feature to help you relax and refocus.

- **Challenges:** Get fun challenges to refresh your mind and break the monotony.
